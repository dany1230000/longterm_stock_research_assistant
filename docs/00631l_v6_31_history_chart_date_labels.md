# 00631L lab v6.31 history chart date labels

v6.31 improves the history/backtest chart date area for phone screens.

## Updated

- The history/backtest chart axis labels now sit inside compact bordered chips.
- The date labels scale down inside their available width, reducing clipping on narrow screens.
- The chart touch-detail panel now uses a two-line layout so the selected date/value and helper text do not compete for one row.
- Stable widget keys cover the start, middle, end axis labels and the touch-detail primary/secondary lines.

## Scope

- No price-history ingestion changes.
- No split-adjustment formula changes.
- No backtest formula changes.
- No investment guidance.
