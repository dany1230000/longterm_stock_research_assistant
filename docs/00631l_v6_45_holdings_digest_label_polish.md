# 00631L v6.45 holdings digest label polish

v6.45 tightens the official holdings digest labels for phone screens.

## What changed

- TX tile title is shortened to `期貨`.
- TSMC tile title is shortened to `台積電`.
- Mix tile title is shortened to `股期現金`.
- Badges remain `TX`, `2330`, and `MIX`.

## What did not change

- Values still come from the official daily holdings snapshot.
- Holdings are still labeled as daily snapshot data, not intraday holdings.
- No source, parser, history, or backtest calculation was changed.
