# 00631L lab v6.23 compact comparison controls

v6.23 reduces vertical clutter in the ETF comparison section.

## What changed

- Comparison action buttons now live in a single horizontal strip.
- Existing actions and stable widget keys are preserved.
- The change is layout-only; comparison filters, basket logic, and chart eligibility are unchanged.

## Why

On phone screens, wrapped action buttons can push the comparison chart too far down. A horizontal action strip keeps controls available while giving more space to the chart and readiness details.

## Scope

- No source-data changes.
- No calculation changes.
- No new ETF eligibility rule.
- No investment guidance.
