# 00631L lab v9.77 - Selected ETF Overview Digest

## Scope

This release clarifies the overview page after switching from 00631L to another
ETF through the top-left symbol search.

## Changes

- 00631L keeps the official holdings and intraday NAV summary on the overview.
- Other selected ETFs now show an ETF-specific data completeness digest instead.
- The digest shows latest price, history row count, price basis, backtest
  readiness, and the selected ETF price-history source.
- The overview no longer suggests that non-00631L ETFs have 00631L official
  holdings or intraday NAV coverage.

## Data Notes

- Non-00631L ETFs currently use verified price history and catalog data.
- Official daily holdings and intraday NAV remain 00631L-focused until each ETF
  receives its own verified source contract.
