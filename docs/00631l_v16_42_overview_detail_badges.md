# 00631L v16.42 Overview Detail Badges

v16.42 localizes the overview detail source-card badges.

## Changed

- `DAY` becomes `日`.
- `LIVE` becomes `盤`.
- `HIS` becomes `歷`.
- `TX` and `AI` remain unchanged because they are meaningful product labels or
  market identifiers.

## Scope

This is a UI label change only. It does not change source status, refresh
frequency, holdings data, intraday NAV, or history calculations.
