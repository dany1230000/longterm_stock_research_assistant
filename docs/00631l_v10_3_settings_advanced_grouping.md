# 00631L lab v10.3 settings advanced grouping

Release tag: `00631l-lab-v10.3-settings-advanced-grouping`

## Goal

Keep the `我的` page focused on daily app use instead of maintenance details.

## Changes

- The first layer keeps account, appearance, selected ETF, local position, and
  ETF data capability controls.
- `資料模式與完整度`, `進階維護診斷`, and `App 上架準備` are grouped under
  `進階設定`.
- App Store planning remains available, but no longer competes with daily
  settings on the first screen.

## Unchanged

- No backend, parser, ETF data, position calculation, or deployment behavior was
  changed.
- No account login or broker connection was added.
