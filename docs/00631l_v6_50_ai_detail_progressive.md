# 00631L v6.50 AI detail progressive disclosure

v6.50 shortens the AI page first screen.

## What changed

- The daily AI briefing remains the first AI section.
- Detailed data snapshot and daily interpretation cards moved into the advanced AI detail panel.
- The visible AI page now opens with the concise briefing and summary before optional detail.

## Data behavior

- Rule-based analysis remains the active provider.
- Analysis text still describes data status, holdings context, price deviation context, and program actions only.
- No external LLM, live TX change, ETF universe expansion, or investment instruction was added.

## Validation focus

- Widget coverage verifies the detailed snapshot and interpretation cards are hidden before expansion.
- The same test verifies those cards appear after opening advanced AI detail.
