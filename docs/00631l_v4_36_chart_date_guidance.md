# 00631L lab v4.36 chart date guidance

v4.36 clarifies chart dates in the history/backtest page.

## What Changed

- The history/backtest page now shows the active chart range above the chart grid.
- The chart guidance explains that the x-axis labels show start, middle, and end dates.

## Why

The chart axis uses compact labels on phone screens. A short range note makes the chart readable without making each chart card taller.
